Picture Day

Saturday, August 29, 2026

Overview

Picture Day will be August 29, 2026 at Chino Hills Community Park. Teams are assigned picture times and should arrive 15-20 minutes before their scheduled time to meet up and sort any last-minute issues.

Make-Up Pictures: Make-up pictures will be during the season. A date has yet to be determined.

Quick Info

Wear Uniforms
Cleats and shin guards are not required.
Bring Team Banner
Please bring your own banner holder stand. Banner needs to be set up before the team gets in line.
Bring Picture Day Form
Forms were given to teams and are available at the tents.

Who Will Be in Pictures?

Team members, coaches, and the team parent are allowed in team pictures.

Buddy Pictures: Buddy pictures are taken with the team. A buddy will join another team's line and take buddy pictures with the person they choose. A separate form must be completed.

Location

Chino Hills Community Park
Meet near the snack bar.
