Empire Strykers Summer Camp

AYSO Region 779 is proud to host the Empire Strykers Summer Camp at Grand Avenue Park. This four-day camp is operated entirely by the Empire Strykers professional indoor soccer organization and led by their professional athletes. Players will enjoy high-energy training, skill development, fun challenges, and a scrimmage - all in a positive, player-first environment.

This camp is open to boys and girls ages 5-17, and all skill levels are welcome.

Empire Strykers Soccer Camps - FAQ

1. What ages can attend the camp?

Strykers camps are open to boys and girls ages 5-17.

Training groups may be separated by age.

2. What skill levels are accepted?

All skill levels are welcome - from beginners to advanced players. Campers are grouped by age and ability to ensure appropriate training.

3. What should my child bring to camp?

Campers should bring:

  • Soccer cleats
  • Shin guards
  • Water bottle
  • Sunscreen
  • Packed snacks (not provided)
  • Extra sweat towel (optional)
4. What are the camp hours?

Typical day camps run from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM.

Some camps operate from 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM (used during extreme heat).

You may drop off your camper, but you must arrive 15 minutes before camp ends for pickup. Staff will not remain on location after camp concludes.

5. Who are the coaches?

All coaches are Empire Strykers professional athletes.

6. Are there any games or tournaments during camp?

Yes - campers participate in a scrimmage during the week.

7. What happens if it rains?

Most camps continue in light rain. If weather conditions become unsafe, sessions may be rescheduled.

8. Is there medical staff or first aid on site?

Yes - camps have trained staff available to handle minor injuries or emergencies.

9. Can parents watch the training sessions?

In many cases, parents are welcome to observe. Some camps may limit access for safety and focus.

10. How do I register and pay?

Registration is completed online through the Empire Strykers registration system.

11. What is the cancellation and refund policy?

No refunds. Campers may be rescheduled to a different date or location.

12. Will players receive a camp T-shirt or gear?

Yes - camps include a T-shirt and soccer ball.
