Practice Overview

Coaches select their own practice day, time, and location - max 2 days a week. If a coach cannot attend, an assistant coach may run practice as long as they hold the same certification level - one of the many reasons every team benefits from having both a head coach and an assistant coach. Weather or field conditions may cancel practice; coordinators will notify coach when extreme heat or city-mandated park closures make fields unusable. Parents should remain at practice to support their player and be available in case of an emergency.

Please do not drop off your child and leave. If you need to step away briefly, communicate directly with your coach.


6U-8U Practice Locations

6U-8U teams may practice at AYSO-approved locations within Chino Hills. Because approval varies by site, coaches should confirm with their Division Coordinator before choosing a practice location. Parks with restroom access are recommended, and coaches should plan ahead for the fall time change, as most neighborhood parks do not have lights and will lose daylight later in the season. If you need help, please reach out to your coordinator.

Practice areas at approved locations are not assigned and operate on a first-come basis, with no division holding priority over another. Please work together if you are sharing an area with other teams.


10U-19U Practice Locations

10U-19U teams use assigned fields for practices. These locations require reservation and cannot be used on a drop-in basis.

Assigned Fields:

  • Community Park
  • Grand Avenue Park

Teams wishing to practice at either location must reserve space by contacting the Assistant Regional Commissioner (ARC). Assigned field usage ensures safe spacing, fair access, and proper coordination across divisions.
