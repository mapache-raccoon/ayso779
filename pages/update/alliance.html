AYSO Alliance

Competitive club soccer with AYSO values, coaching, and affordability

Tryout-Based Club Pathway

Alliance brings club-level soccer into AYSO, combining competitive play with the values families expect from our program: positive coaching, meaningful playing opportunities, and a more affordable model than many traditional club programs.

In Chino Hills, Alliance operates as the competitive club pathway within AYSO for players ready to move beyond EXTRA. Teams play a year-round schedule in the Coast Soccer League (CSL) and also compete in AYSO and open tournaments, with opportunities for college showcases and State Cup participation.

Alliance is for players and families who are ready for a higher level of competition and commitment. It should be treated as a player's primary athletic obligation after academics, including travel for league and tournament play that may range from local competition to longer-distance events.

What Alliance Is
  • Club-level competition against strong league and tournament opponents.
  • AYSO values including positive coaching and guaranteed playing time.
  • Affordability at a fraction of traditional club soccer costs.
  • Dedicated coaches committed to long-term player development.
How It Operates
  • Tryout-based selection for players seeking a higher competitive level.
  • Year-long commitment with practices, league play, and tournaments.
  • Travel for CSL matches, AYSO tournaments, and open events.
  • Pathway opportunities including college showcase features and State Cup eligibility.
Alliance is designed for players who have reached the top of their current AYSO pathway and are ready for the next step in a more demanding soccer environment.
