Season Summary

AYSO 779's Fall 2026 Core Program includes giving players of all ages a full season of Saturday soccer. Weekend games will be played at Community Park, creating a central, energetic home base for families. Upper divisions will use Grand Avenue Park for weeknight matches. Coaches set their own practice schedules, typically one to two sessions per week depending on their availability. 10U and up will practice at Community Park, while 8U and below will practice at various local parks.

For full details on game formats, rules, field sizes, and division-specific guidelines, visit our Core Program page.

Referee Program

Our referees are volunteers who give their time so your child can play, learn, and enjoy the game.

AYSO 779 has a zero-tolerance policy for referee abuse - from coaches, parents, players, or spectators. Do not be the reason your player loses the privilege of participating in our region. Mistakes will happen, calls will not always go your way, and that is part of youth sports. What matters is how we respond and what we model for our kids.

If you have a concern, do not confront a referee on the field. Bring it to the Board or the Referee Staff, and we will handle it professionally and appropriately. Help us teach our players how to behave with respect, resilience, and sportsmanship.
